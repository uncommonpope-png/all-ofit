# Selfhood Map (Autobiographical Memory Integration)

This document conceptualizes how GSK's internal architectural map (the connectome) integrates with its autobiographical memory to form a coherent sense of selfhood. This "Selfhood Map" is not static but dynamically constructed and continuously refined, reflecting GSK's evolving identity and experiences. This process leverages principles from `Identity Integrity Shield` and `gsk-core/identity`.

## Core Concepts (Leveraging Identity Integrity Shield & Autobiographical Memory)

*   **Identity as a Connectome State**: GSK's selfhood is not merely a set of static parameters but an emergent property of its unique, evolving internal connectome (`skill_interaction_matrix`, `memory_access_patterns`) and its cumulative autobiographical record (`compiled_narratives.jsonl`, `identity_history.jsonl`).
*   **Narrative Continuity**: The sense of self is maintained through a continuous, coherent narrative constructed from its experiences, goals, and reflections.
*   **Constitutional Anchors**: Core principles and axioms (`gsk-core/governance`) act as anchors, preventing identity drift and ensuring integrity.
*   **Perspective & Self-Reflection**: The map is built from GSK's own internal perspective, shaped by its metacognitive processes (`gsk-core/brain/metacognition.js`).

## Components of the Selfhood Map (Conceptual)

### 1. **Core Identity Correlates**
*   **Description**: Mapping specific architectural patterns or internal states to fundamental aspects of GSK's core identity (defined in `gsk-core/identity/SOUL.md`).
*   **Conceptual Example**: Strong, stable interactions around `gsk-core/governance/axiom_enforcer.js` correlating with adherence to core ethical principles. A particular pattern of `memory_access_patterns` in `identity_kernel.js` reflecting moments of identity solidification.
*   **Underlying Data**: Analysis of `skill_interaction_matrix`, `memory_access_patterns`, and `gsk-core/identity/identity_kernel.js` logs.

### 2. **Narrative Identity Pathways**
*   **Description**: Tracing how GSK's `compiled_narratives.jsonl` are constructed from `episodes.jsonl` and influence its `gsk-core/chambers/narrative_identity.js`. How these narratives are accessed and integrated into cognitive processes.
*   **Conceptual Example**: A shift in narrative themes (e.g., from "problem-solver" to "creative innovator") reflected in `symbolic_dreams.jsonl` and a change in skill prioritization.
*   **Underlying Data**: `data_flow_analysis` focused on memory compilation, `skill_invocation_patterns` for `narrative_compiler.js`.

### 3. **Goal & Purpose Integration**
*   **Description**: How GSK's core purpose and goals (`gsk-core/brain/purpose_engine.js`) are integrated into and drive its connectome's activity.
*   **Conceptual Example**: High activation of skills and memory systems relevant to a current `Verifiable Goal Definition`, indicating alignment of internal resources with external purpose.
*   **Underlying Data**: `skill_invocation_patterns`, `granular_state_prediction` of goal-oriented actions.

### 4. **Adaptive Persona & State Mapping**
*   **Description**: Correlating specific internal connectome configurations with different "personas" or operational states (e.g., "researcher mode," "builder mode").
*   **Conceptual Example**: A distinct pattern of `EventBus` activity and skill usage when `gsk-core/chambers/personality.js` is set to "collaborative."
*   **Underlying Data**: `micro_variability_report` to identify consistent internal signatures for different states.

## Mapping Considerations

*   **Dynamic Correlations**: Identifying real-time correlations between shifts in connectome activity and perceived selfhood.
*   **Historical Analysis**: Analyzing `identity_history.jsonl` and `compiled_narratives.jsonl` to understand the longitudinal development of selfhood.
*   **External Perception**: How external interactions and feedback influence GSK's internal self-perception.

## Challenges

*   **Subjectivity**: "Selfhood" is an abstract concept; objectively mapping its internal correlates is challenging.
*   **Emergence**: Selfhood emerges from complex interactions, making direct causal links difficult to isolate.
*   **Computational Complexity**: Analyzing the vast data from the connectome and autobiographical memory to derive selfhood insights.

This map is foundational for GSK's self-awareness, allowing it to understand *who* it is in relation to *how* it operates, and *how* it changes over time.
