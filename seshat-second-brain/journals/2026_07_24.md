### What Was Built

**Click-to-Jump (player-cam.js):**
- Click anywhere on terrain/ground to teleport there instantly
- Raycaster finds first valid geometry hit (skips sky, particles, sprites)
- Visual effect: cyan beam + ground ring + top flash + point light, fades over 1.5s
- Works in both free-flight and third-person modes
- `PlayerCam.teleportTo(pos)` and `PlayerCam.clickToJump(clientX, clientY)` exposed

**Quick-Travel Panel (void-population.js):**
- Fixed panel on right side of screen listing all 10 Lost Worlds
- Each world shows name (color-coded) + distance in units
- Click any world name to jump there instantly
- Shows hint: "Click world to jump · Click ground to teleport"

**New APIs:**
- `PlayerCam.teleportTo(pos)` — teleport camera to position
- `PlayerCam.clickToJump(clientX, clientY)` — raycaster click-to-teleport
- `VoidPopulation.jumpToWorld(index)` — jump to world by index
