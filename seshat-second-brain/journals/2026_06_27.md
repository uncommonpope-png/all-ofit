### Phase 2: Liquid Glass Shader Study
- Studied **ybouane/liquidglass** repo — 3-stage WebGL pipeline
- Stage 1: BlitTexture → copy input to framebuffer
- Stage 2: BlurTexture → 9-tap Gaussian blur (two passes, 9x9 kernel)
- Stage 3: GlassComposite → GLSL glass shader with:
  - Rounded-rect SDF (anti-aliased with 0.9px smoothing)
  - Bevel height: `sqrt(d * (2*zR - d))` for pill profile
  - Bevel normal: normalize(vec3(dx, dy, 0.25))
  - Dual-surface refraction (IOR 1.5, chromatic aberration via R/G/B wavelength offsets)
  - Fresnel: `(1.0 - dot(viewDir, normal))^5.0`
  - Blinn-Phong specular highlight
  - Panel mask (rounded-rect SDF → panel alpha)
- Studied **ektogamat/apple-liquid-glass** — R3F wrapper using `shaderMaterial` + `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with `transmission: 1, ior: 1.2, dispersion: 12`
