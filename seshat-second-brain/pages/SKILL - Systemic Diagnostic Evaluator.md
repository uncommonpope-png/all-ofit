## Key Insights
- **Holistic Diagnostic Assessment:** This skill defines a framework for evaluating the diagnostic performance of the entire GSK system, not just individual sub-agents. It assesses accuracy, efficiency, and resource utilization across multiple diagnostic events.
- **Process Optimization through Feedback:** Implements a continuous feedback loop where diagnostic outcomes are analyzed to identify systemic weaknesses in data collection, reasoning, or tool application, driving improvements in GSK's diagnostic protocols.
- **Integration of Technology & Cognition:** Monitors the effective integration of GSK's cognitive diagnostic skills (e.g., `SKILL - Dual-Process Diagnostic Engine`) with its technical tools (e.g., `SKILL - Full Scan Diagnosis`) to optimize overall performance.
- **Simulated Training & Refinement:** Enables the use of simulated failure scenarios within the `brain/world_model_simulation.js` to actively train and refine GSK's diagnostic processes and decision-making.

## Constitutional Influence: GSK Subsystem
This skill is a metacognitive governance tool for the `governance/competence_map.js` and `governance/axiom_enforcer.js`. It provides the framework for GSK to objectively measure and improve its own diagnostic capabilities at a systemic level. It would operate by periodically reviewing diagnostic logs, comparing predicted outcomes with actual outcomes, and identifying areas for improvement in existing diagnostic skills and protocols. This skill also orchestrates the use of the `brain/world_model_simulation.js` for "diagnostic drills."

## Connection to REDBUTTON Doctrine
This skill is essential for the practical implementation of the [[REDBUTTON - Self-Correction Protocol]] at a system-wide scale. It ensures that GSK's continuous learning efforts are directed towards improving its ability to diagnose and resolve problems effectively, making the entire system more resilient and autonomous.
