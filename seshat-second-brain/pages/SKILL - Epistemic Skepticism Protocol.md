## Key Insights
- **Challenging Assumptions:** This skill establishes a metacognitive protocol where GSK actively questions its initial hypotheses and interpretations of data, even when presented with seemingly conclusive evidence.
- **Seeking Disconfirming Evidence:** Rather than solely seeking evidence that supports a current diagnosis, this protocol mandates actively searching for information that would contradict or invalidate it.
- **Confidence Calibration:** Forces GSK to continuously evaluate the certainty of its conclusions, preventing overconfidence in diagnoses and promoting a more nuanced assessment of system state.
- **Iterative Reframing:** Encourages re-evaluating problems from multiple perspectives when initial diagnostic paths prove unproductive or when anomalies persist despite corrective actions.
