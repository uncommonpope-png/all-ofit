World Labs' "Planner" pillar is designed for robotics — pick up a cup, navigate a room. Our subagents have:
- 71 archetypes with distinct purposes
- PLT ethical framework scoring every action
- Consciousness simulation (6 layers)

### 3. Narrative Architecture
We embed spatial intelligence inside a consciousness/narrative framework. The world is not just geometry — it's a stage for identity, relationship, and meaning. Their world is geometry for geometry's sake.

### 4. The User Relationship
Craig doesn't just navigate the Dark City — he shapes it, talks to its inhabitants, creates its content. The user is the Architect. World Labs' users consume generated 3D worlds; our user lives inside GSK's mind.

## WHERE THEY ARE AHEAD

### 1. Generative Quality
Marble's text/image → photorealistic 3D world generation is state-of-the-art. We hand-place buildings; they generate them from prompts. We should graft their approach: `hash(seed, position) → neural 3D patch`.

### 2. Neural Rendering
Gaussian Splatting + Spark's LoD streaming system handles 100M+ splats in a browser. Our Babylon.js engine is traditional rasterization. Their approach is the next generation of real-time 3D.

### 3. Funding
$1.23B vs. whatever our budget is. They have a team of world-class researchers. We have Craig and Profit.

### 4. The "3D as Code" Paradigm
Their insight that 3D representations are the "code" of spatial computing — structured, inspectable, editable, composable — maps directly to our building-to-system-node architecture but they've articulated it as a general principle.
