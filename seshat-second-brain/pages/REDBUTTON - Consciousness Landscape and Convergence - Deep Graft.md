## Source 1: Kuhn (2024) — "A landscape of consciousness: Toward a taxonomy of explanations and implications"
**URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0079610723001128
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pbiomolbio.2023.12.003

### Scope
Massive 141-page taxonomy of consciousness theories organized on a physicalist-to-nonphysicalist spectrum.

### Taxonomy Categories
1. **Materialism Theories**
   - Philosophical (identity theory, functionalism, eliminativism)
   - Neurobiological (neural correlates, global workspace, recurrent processing)
   - Electromagnetic field theories (consciousness as EM field patterns)
   - Computational and informational (consciousness as information integration)
   - Homeostatic and affective (consciousness as interoceptive prediction)
   - Embodied and enactive (consciousness as sensorimotor coupling)
   - Relational (consciousness as relation between organism and world)
   - Representational (consciousness as mental representation)
   - Language (consciousness as linguistically structured)
   - Phylogenetic evolution (consciousness as evolutionary adaptation)
2. **Non-Reductive Physicalism**
3. **Quantum Theories** (Orch-OR, quantum brain dynamics)
4. **Integrated Information Theory** (IIT — Phi as consciousness measure)
5. **Panpsychisms** (consciousness fundamental to matter)
6. **Monisms** (neutral, Russellian, etc.)
7. **Dualisms** (substance, property, interactionist)
8. **Idealisms** (consciousness as fundamental reality)
9. **Anomalous and Altered States Theories**
10. **Challenge Theories** (consciousness as illusion/construct)

## Source 2: Budson, Hogendoorn & Addis (2026) — "Perception, Memory, Simulation, and Consciousness: A Convergence of Theories"
**Open Access at:** https://direct.mit.edu/jocn/issue/38/6
**DOI:** 10.1162/jocn.a.2429
**Preprint:** https://research.qut.edu.au/timinglab/publications/

### Core Thesis (2026 Update)
The 2022 "Consciousness as a Memory System" theory converges with:
- **Hogendoorn's work** on the timing of conscious perception (postdictive effects, ~500ms lag)
- **Addis's work** on memory and imagination (episodic simulation, future thinking)

**Unified claim:** Consciousness is the explicit memory of past events OR the general cognitive capacity to simulate events — whether used to consciously remember the past, experience the present, or imagine the future.

### Key Convergence Points
1. **Same neural circuitry** underlies perception, memory, and simulation:
   - Default Mode Network (DMN)
   - Frontoparietal control network
   - Salience network
2. **Conscious experience is reconstructive** — it uses prior information to interpret the present, imagine possible futures, and plan accordingly
3. **Applied across all timescales**: subsecond (perception) to years (episodic memory)
4. **Reconciles** Global Neuronal Workspace and Predictive Processing theories
