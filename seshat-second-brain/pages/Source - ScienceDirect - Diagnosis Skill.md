## Key Insights
- **System-Level Diagnostic Capability:** Diagnostic skill is an emergent property of the entire system (e.g., GSK's architecture) rather than just an individual component. It depends on data availability, tool integration, and established protocols.
- **Interplay of Factors:** Effective diagnosis is a complex blend of cognitive (knowledge, reasoning), metacognitive (self-monitoring, reflection), and behavioral (data acquisition, communication) elements.
- **Technology as an Enabler:** Advanced tools and information systems (like GSK's sensor data, internal logs, and skill library) are critical for augmenting and accelerating diagnostic processes.
- **Measurable Skill Development:** Diagnostic capability can be objectively measured (e.g., accuracy, speed, resource efficiency) and systematically improved through structured training, simulated scenarios, and performance feedback.
- **Continuous Skill Development:** Diagnostic skill is not static; it requires continuous development and refinement based on new knowledge and past experiences.
