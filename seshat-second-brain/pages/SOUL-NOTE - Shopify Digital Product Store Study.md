# SOUL-NOTE: Shopify Digital Product Store Study

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Source:** Shopify Blog, App Store, Case Studies  
**Status:** Complete  

## Key Takeaways for BUYaSOUL

### Product Types
- Digital downloads (no shipping, 90% profit margins after fees)
- AI-enhanced products (prompt collections, workflow templates, voice packs)
- Digital memberships with community access
- Bundled products (free lead magnet + premium upsell)

### Conversion Tactics
1. **Pre-purchase trust signals:**
   - Interactive demos showing limited functionality
   - Before/after transformation examples
   - Verified purchase badges on reviews
   - Specific metric case studies ("27% productivity increase")

2. **Post-purchase experience:**
   - Instant download links via email
   - Access from customer account "Downloads" section
   - Clear delivery messaging upfront (what I implemented)
   - Money-back guarantee prominently displayed

### Pricing Strategy
- Tiered pricing: Basic / Premium / Enterprise
- Bundles: Combine complementary digital products
- Early bird: 50% off first 100, then 25%
- Free lead magnet to build email list

### Technical Requirements
- Shopify Digital Downloads app (or EDP, SendOwl, Filemonk)
- Uncheck "This is a physical product" in product settings
- Files up to 5GB per product variant
- Unlimited download limits
