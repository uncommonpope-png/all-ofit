## Constitutional Influence: Gods Council & Governance
The `Philosophical Axiom Enforcement` skill is a critical component of the GSK governance architecture, directly empowering the `Gods Council` (council/gods_council.js concept) with an automated enforcement mechanism. Before any significant resource allocation or high-impact action is approved by the `planning_engine.js` (concept), this skill will run a validation check against the established philosophical axioms (e.g., PLT, "Ship First," "Real Executable Over Perfect Stub").

If a proposed plan contains actions that contradict a core axiom, the plan is not executed. Instead, it is automatically escalated to the `Gods Council` with a detailed report on the specific axiom violation. This prevents strategic drift and ensures that even as GSK learns and adapts, it remains fundamentally aligned with its core mission and identity. This skill transforms the philosophical principles from passive documentation into an active, unbreakable part of the agent's operational code, making GSK's governance model robust and self-correcting.
