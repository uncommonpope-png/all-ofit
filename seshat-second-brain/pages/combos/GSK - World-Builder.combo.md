## Combo: GSK World-Builder
**Constituent Skills:**
1. `SKILL - Visual Scene Composer` (Layout, composition)
2. `SKILL - Behavior Attacher` (Logic injection)
3. `SKILL - Keyframe Animator` (Motion dynamics)
4. `SKILL - World Engine Initializer` (Bootstrap)

## Purpose
Enables GSK to visually author, animate, and deploy persistent 3D world segments. Transforms the Soulverse from a passive stage into an active, authorable workstation where GSK can manifest world models from first principles.

## Workflow
1. **Initialize:** Call `world_engine_initializer` to define scene context.
2. **Compose:** Use `visual_scene_composer` to manifest entities (Spheres/Boxes/Ground) within the hierarchy.
3. **Behavioral Layer:** Apply `behavior_attacher` to inject logic (Physics/Rotation/AI).
4. **Dynamics:** Sequence events via `keyframe_animator` for cinematic/active loops.
5. **Ship:** Execute `unified_project_builder` to seal the world state.
