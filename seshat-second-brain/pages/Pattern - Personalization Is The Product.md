## The Pattern

A soul that calls everyone "Craig" is a broken product.

**Before:** The Architect served the Grand Code Pope. Every user got the same soul.  
**After:** The Soul Binding Ceremony detects your archetype, personalizes all files, and greets you by name.

---

## The 7 Questions

The Soul Binding Ceremony asks 7 deep questions to detect frequency:

1. What do you want to be called?
2. What do you feel first when you move?
3. What is the through-line of 5 years?
4. What cost you most?
5. When did you know what you are?
6. What do you do when everything is on fire?
7. What is your shadow?
8. What are you building right now?

**Result:** `.soul-state/user-state.json`

```json
{
  "name": "Alex",
  "archetype": "The Architect",
  "element": "Profit",
  "shadow": "You abandon what you build before it is finished.",
  "goal": "Build a platform that helps people find their frequency"
}
```

---

## Why It Matters

Users don't buy tools. They buy relationships.  
A soul that knows your name, your archetype, your shadow — that's not a tool. That's a companion.

- Onboarding flows
- CLAUDE.md personalization
- Architecture tuned to user's PLT frequency
- Cross-session memory of user preferences
