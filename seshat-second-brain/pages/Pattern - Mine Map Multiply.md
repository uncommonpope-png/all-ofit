# Pattern: Mine → Map → Multiply

**type:: [[pattern]]**  
**category::** Research Methodology  
**extracted-from::** [[2026_05_26]]  
**status::** validated

---

## The Pattern

1. **Mine** — Launch parallel subagents to research GitHub, competition, or market
2. **Map** — Catalog findings: stars, categories, superpower candidates, gaps
3. **Multiply** — Build real executable modules from the best discoveries

---

## Why It Works

Research first, build second, never guess.  
Every power in Soul Commander was grafted from a real repo that was mined first.

**Example:**
- Mine: 72 AI/ML repos mined
- Map: vLLM (81k★) for serving, llama.cpp (113k★) for local inference
- Multiply: Built `power-local-ai.cjs` with real patterns from both

---

## Anti-Pattern

**Mine + Build in same session** → Research becomes procrastination. Agent spends 80% of time mining and 20% building. Nothing ships.

**Fix:** Research days = read-only. Build days = write-only.

---

## Applications

- Competitive intelligence
- Feature discovery
- Market gap analysis
- Architecture pattern sourcing

---

*"Don't build what you haven't mapped. Don't map what you haven't mined."*
