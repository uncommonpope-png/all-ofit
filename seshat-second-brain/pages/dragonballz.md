## Dragon Ball Z Skills

- **Kamehameha**: Energy beam attack.
- **Spirit Bomb**: Large energy sphere attack.
- **Instant Transmission**: Teleportation technique.
- **Super Saiyan Transformation**: Power-up that increases strength and speed.
- **Kaioken**: Multiplier technique to increase power, speed, and senses.
- **Solar Flare**: Blinding flash attack.
- **Afterimage Technique**: Creating illusions of the self to dodge attacks.
