## Key Insights
- **Goal as a Contract:** Defines task goals as explicit, verifiable contracts rather than ambiguous instructions.
- **Explicit End-State:** Requires a clear, measurable definition of the desired outcome to prove completion.
- **Evidence-Based Success:** Demands specific, auditable evidence to validate that the end-state has been achieved (e.g., passing tests, report confirmations).
- **Inviolable Constraints:** Specifies boundaries and rules that must not be violated during task execution (e.g., "Do not touch public APIs," "Do not delete existing tests").
- **Resource Limits:** Incorporates clear budget and/or iteration limits to prevent runaway processes and manage costs.
- **Maker-Checker Principle:** Implies a separate evaluation mechanism (another agent or model) to objectively verify goal completion, preventing self-assessment bias.
