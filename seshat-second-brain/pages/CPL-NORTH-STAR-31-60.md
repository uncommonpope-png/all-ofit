# CPL / GSK — Phases 31-60 + Competitive Landscape

## Competitive landscape (the white space)
Three real adjacent categories — **nobody combines all three** as a spatial world with visible agent-authored change + a real book economy:
1. **Embodied AI-NPC platforms** — Convai, Charisma.ai, Inworld AI (Unity/Unreal/web/XR; see/hear/speak/act). Infra, not destinations. Gap: no commerce layer, no visitor-visible code execution.
2. **AI companion apps** — Character.AI, Replika, Soulmate, Soul Machines "Digital People" (92% more effective / 85% more engaging than chatbots per their claim). Chat-companions, not spatial worlds that sell books.
3. **AI-assisted publishing tools** — MS Copilot, Sudowrite, "AI book publisher" services. Stop at the document; no world around the catalog.
**Our white space = intersection of all three**: embodied agent (1) + personality/economy hook (2) + real product catalog (3), expressed as a *spatial environment* visitors explore. Currently empty. Won't stay empty (MCP adoption is moving fast) → Phases 41+ are about moving first.
