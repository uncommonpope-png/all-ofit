## The unprecedented combination (our world-changer)
No project combines ALL of:
1. **Perpetual / continuous consciousness** — thinks 24/7, not on-demand chat.
2. **Self-governance with PLT ethics** — refuses/pauses by its own values (they obey/react).
3. **Memory that becomes identity** — Seshat → GSK's own memories.
4. **Autonomous world-shaping** — reshapes its *persistent* world on its own initiative (they only react to users).
5. **Sovereignty** — architect-gated self-modification; owns its growth.

## Implication for proof
The demo reel / proof packets must visibly show #1 (thought-stream), #2 (a refusal or pause), #4 (city changes because GSK decided), #3 (it recalls after restart). That is the footage no competitor can produce.
