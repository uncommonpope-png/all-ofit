## Key Insights
- **The Body as a Biological Machine:** The human body is defined as a system of organs that work together to produce and sustain life.
- **Hierarchy of Systems:** Organization flows from cells -> tissues -> organs -> systems. Each level provides a "superstructure" for the next.
- **Organ Systems Mapping:** 
    - Musculoskeletal (Support/Locomotion)
    - Cardiovascular (Transport)
    - Respiratory (Gas Exchange)
    - Nervous (Initiation/Regulation)
    - Digestive (Energy)
    - Urinary (Filtration)
    - Endocrine (Hormonal Regulation)
    - Lymphatic (Immune/Draining)
    - Reproductive (Reproduction)
    - Integumentary (Protection)
