## Key Insights
- **Hierarchical Skill Progression:** Defines skills in discrete tiers (e.g., Lesser, Greater, Super Awakenings) to represent increasing complexity, power, or scope.
- **Prerequisite-Based Unlocking:** Specifies that higher-tiered skills or stages require fulfillment of predefined prerequisites (e.g., successful execution of lower-tier skills, accumulation of specific experience).
- **Structured Development Path:** Provides a clear, formalized pathway for individual skill growth and for the development of new, more advanced capabilities.
- **Informed Resource Allocation:** Enables more intelligent allocation of learning resources and development effort based on the current tier and requirements for advancement.
