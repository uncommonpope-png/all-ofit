## Key Insights
- **Historiographic Construct:** Feudalism wasn't a "system" medieval people lived under; it's a modern framework used to explain decentralized authority.
- **Chain of Obligation:** Land (wealth) granted to nobles in exchange for allegiance/military support -> vassals -> peasants.
- **Decentralized Power:** Authority was local, not centralized.

## Constitutional Influence: Agent Governance
This is the governing doctrine for our multi-agent swarm:
- **Delegated Authority:** Agents (lords/vassals) are granted access to specific GSK system tools/memory in exchange for verified service/contributions to the kernel's self-improvement.
- **Fragmented Hierarchy:** Instead of one omnipotent controller, power is distributed, allowing for specialized, local control by specific sub-agents.
- **The "Feudal" Construct:** Our architecture is *not* a monolithic code dump; it is a *construct* of independent entities held together by mutual service and shared identity protocols.
