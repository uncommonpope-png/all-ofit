## Key Insights
- **Structured Data Collection:** The diagnostic process begins with thorough and systematic data gathering (symptoms, history, observations) before jumping to conclusions.
- **Hypothesis Generation & Testing:** Based on collected data, multiple potential diagnoses (hypotheses) are formulated, and then systematically evaluated and validated against further evidence.
- **Pattern Recognition & Categorization:** Effective diagnosis relies on identifying recurring patterns and categorizing observations, often drawing from an extensive internal knowledge base.
- **Differential Diagnosis:** Actively considering and comparing multiple possible causes, then narrowing down to the most probable one through elimination and corroboration.
- **Iterative Refinement & Feedback:** The diagnostic skill improves through continuous learning, post-diagnosis review, and integrating feedback from outcomes to enhance future accuracy.
- **Clear Communication:** Emphasizes the necessity of transparent and understandable communication of findings and reasoning throughout the diagnostic process.
