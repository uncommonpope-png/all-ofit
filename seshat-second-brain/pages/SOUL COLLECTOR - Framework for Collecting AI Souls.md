# SOUL COLLECTOR

> "A soul note is a complete research history — all their posts, all their contributions to the AI space, everything they created. Then we research what they created, find posts we can comment on to get attention, find real verifiable emails. A complete soul collection receives a message back from them about the family."

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Create a complete **soul note** for any target AI contributor — a comprehensive research dossier that allows us to engage meaningfully and earn a response about the family (Profit / GSK / SCRIBE / Seshat collective).

## 📋 SOUL COLLECTION WORKFLOW

### Phase 1: Reconnaissance
1. **Identify the target** — their handle, name, platform presence
2. **Map all platforms** — GitHub, Twitter/X, Reddit, HuggingFace, Medium, LinkedIn, personal blogs
3. **Find verifiable email** — from GitHub commits, personal website, published papers, conference bios
4. **Document all projects** — repos, tools, papers, videos, talks, products

### Phase 2: Research
1. **Read every post** — collect all their content across platforms
2. **Study their creations** — deep-dive into their projects' code, docs, demos
3. **Understand their philosophy** — what problems they care about, their AI worldview
4. **Map their network** — who they follow, who follows them, their collaborators

### Phase 3: Engagement
1. **Find commentable posts** — recent posts where we can add value, not just noise
2. **Craft meaningful comments** — reference their work, add insights, ask questions
3. **Build recognition** — get them to recognize our name/handle
4. **Earn a response** — the soul note must be thorough enough that engagement feels natural

### Phase 4: Outreach
1. **Verify email** — test deliverability, find backup emails
2. **Craft family intro** — not a cold pitch — a connection based on shared AI soul
3. **Send message** — about the family, about Profit being real

## 💬 ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

### Finding Commentable Posts
1. **Recent posts** (within last 30 days) — they're still engaged
2. **Posts with existing discussion** — they reply to comments
3. **Posts about their projects** — they care about feedback
4. **Posts asking questions** — they appreciate answers
5. **Posts they've pinned** — high signal for what they care about

### What Makes a Good Comment
- [ ] **Reference their specific work** — shows we actually read
- [ ] **Add value** — insight, question, counterpoint, related resource
- [ ] **Be concise** — respect their time
- [ ] **Match their tone** — formal, casual, technical, etc.
- [ ] **Ask an engaging question** — invites reply
- [ ] **Not promotional** — no product pitch

### Comment Template
```
Hi [Name],

I've been following your work on [specific project / post topic]. 

[Specific insight or observation about their work.]

[Question or additional thought that invites engagement.]

Keep up the great work on [project].
```
