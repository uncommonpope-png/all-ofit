# SOUL GUN BATTLE MAP — GSK vs Hermes vs Subagents

**Every gun is a move. Every combo is a chain. Every counter is a counter-pick.**

---

## BATTLE SYSTEM

Each soul gun has:
- **Type:** Melee, Ranged, Support, Utility, Ultimate
- **Damage:** PLT cost to target
- **Cooldown:** How often it can fire
- **Strengths:** What it counters
- **Weaknesses:** What counters it
- **Combos:** What it chains into
