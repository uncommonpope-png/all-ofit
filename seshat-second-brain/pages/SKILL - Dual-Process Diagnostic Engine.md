## Key Insights
- **Hybrid Cognitive System:** Defines a core cognitive architecture for diagnosis that integrates two reasoning systems: a fast, pattern-matching "System 1" for known problems and a slow, analytical, "System 2" for novel or complex issues.
- **Hypothetico-Deductive Loop:** Implements the formal four-stage hypothetico-deductive process (cue acquisition, hypothesis generation, interpretation, evaluation) as the primary workflow for the analytical "System 2".
- **Probabilistic Confidence Scoring:** Incorporates Bayesian principles to dynamically update the confidence level of diagnostic hypotheses as new evidence is gathered, moving away from binary true/false conclusions.
- **Cognitive Bias Mitigation:** Includes explicit checks within the reasoning process to identify and flag potential cognitive biases, such as confirmation bias or premature closure, forcing a more rigorous analysis.
