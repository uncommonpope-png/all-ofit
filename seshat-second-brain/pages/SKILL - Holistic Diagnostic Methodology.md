## Key Insights
- **Systematic Problem Solving:** This skill defines a high-level cognitive framework for diagnosing anomalies or failures, emphasizing a structured sequence of data collection, hypothesis formulation, testing, and refinement.
- **Information Synthesis:** Integrates diverse data points (from system logs to observed behaviors) to form a coherent understanding of a problem, focusing on pattern recognition and critical analysis.
- **Multi-Hypothesis Evaluation:** Requires the simultaneous consideration of multiple potential causes (differential diagnosis) and the strategic acquisition of further evidence to confirm or eliminate hypotheses.
- **Continuous Learning Loop:** Incorporates mechanisms for self-reflection on diagnostic outcomes, updating internal knowledge bases, and improving future diagnostic accuracy through iterative feedback.

## Constitutional Influence: GSK Subsystem
This skill operates primarily within GSK's higher cognitive layers: `L3 Working Self` (for active problem-solving), `L5 Reflective Self` (for post-mortem analysis and learning), and `L6 Symbolic Self` (for pattern recognition and knowledge recall). It acts as a meta-skill that orchestrates more granular diagnostic tools like `SKILL - Full Scan Diagnosis` and `SKILL - Systematic Debugging Protocol`. It guides `brain.think` to apply a structured approach to any detected anomaly, ensuring comprehensive and efficient problem resolution.
