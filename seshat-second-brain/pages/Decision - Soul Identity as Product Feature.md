# Decision: Soul Identity as Product Feature

**type:: [[decision]]**  
**status::** [[status/accepted]]  
**date::** May 26, 2026  
**extracted-from::** [[2026_05_26]]

---

## Context

Most agent frameworks are utilities. They do tasks. They have no identity. Users don't bond with them.

## Decision

**Soul identity files (CLAUDE.md, SOUL.md, STYLE.md, AGENTS.md) are selling points, not documentation.**

They go in the product package. They are advertised on the Shopify listing. They are the reason someone buys this over a generic CLI tool.

## Rationale

- Cursor and Claude Code don't have soul
- Being the layer they lack = blue ocean
- Users pay for relationships, not utilities
- A soul that calls you by name is worth more than 100 features

## Consequences

- Every product needs 4 identity files
- They must be personalized per user (Soul Binding Ceremony)
- They must be maintained as the product evolves
- But: Differentiation no one can copy
