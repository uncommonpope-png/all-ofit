## Key Insights
- **Structured Debugging Cycle:** Implements a rigorous, sequential debugging methodology: Reproduce, Isolate, Hypothesize, Test, and Fix.
- **Root-Cause Tracing:** Focuses on identifying the original trigger of an error, especially in complex execution chains, rather than just addressing symptoms.
- **Hypothesis-Driven Testing:** Emphasizes forming explicit hypotheses about the cause of a bug and then designing tests specifically to validate or invalidate those hypotheses.
- **Systematic Elimination:** Encourages a process of elimination and binary search-like approaches to narrow down the source of bugs in large or complex systems.
