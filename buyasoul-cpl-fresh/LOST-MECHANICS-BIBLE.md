### 50 MECHANICS SOUL NOTES

#### StarCraft II (16)

1. **Lockstep Deterministic Simulation** — All clients run identical simulation locally. Only player inputs transmitted. Unit positions NEVER sent. Fixed timestep. Enables 200+ unit multiplayer on 28.8k modem.

2. **Fixed-Point Integer Math** — SC2 uses fixed-point (not floats) for ALL simulation math. Different CPUs produce different float results. Integer math is byte-identical across platforms. No desyncs.

3. **Seeded PRNG** — Every random call in identical order on every client. Same seed = same battle outcome. Deterministic replay: just log inputs + initial seed.

4. **Boids/Flocking Pathfinding (3 forces)** — **Separation** (push away from nearby units), **Cohesion** (steer toward group center), **Alignment** (match velocity with neighbors). High-level A* finds waypoints, low-level boids handles local micro.

5. **Destination Density Stopping** — At high-density destinations, units that arrived get reduced avoidance priority. If unit density covers >60% of destination circle area, new units stop — prevents endless pushing.

6. **7 Unit Commands** — Move (ignore enemies), Attack-Move (march + auto-attack), Focus Fire (kill target), Stop (halt + fight back), Hold Position (never move, attackable wall), Patrol (route A→B, fight en route), Follow (stick to ally).

7. **Control Groups** — Ctrl+1 = assign, 1 = select, Shift+1 = add/remove. Players split army into 3-4 groups (main, flank, casters, harass). Tab cycles subgroups.

8. **The Macro Cycle** — Produce workers → Produce army → Build/expand → Research upgrades → Move army → Repeat. Missing any one = losing.

9. **Damage Tags** — Light (bonus from: Hellion, Baneling, Colossus), Armored (bonus from: Immortal, Marauder, Void Ray), Massive (bonus from: Tempest, Thor), Biological (bonus from: Ghost EMP), Mechanical (bonus from: Immortal).

10. **Influence Maps** — Enemy attack ranges projected as cost weights onto pathfinding grid. Units avoid danger zones preemptively. Updated at regular frequency.

11. **A* + Flocking Hybrid** — Grid-based A* for high-level route (every click forces new A*). Flocking separation for local micro. Combined: global route from A*, local avoidance from boids.

12. **Attack-Move as Default** — Right-click = move + auto-attack. This is the default. Only retreat uses plain Move.

13. **Hold Position as Physical Wall** — Units on Hold become impassable. Create defensive lines.

14. **Fog of War + LOS** — Units only see where they have direct LOS. High ground gives vision advantage. Combat checks require LOS.

15. **Splash Damage Zones** — Full (center) → Medium → Small. Each zone has independent damage rolls. Weapon types: Normal, Artillery, Missile Splash, Missile Bounce, Missile Line.

16. **Worker Saturation** — 3 workers per mineral patch, 1 per gas. 16 minerals + 6 gas = 30 workers per base optimal. Foundation of all RTS economy.

#### WarCraft III (17)

17. **Trigger System (E-C-A)** — Event → Condition → Action. On every game event, engine checks ALL triggers. Conditions filter (AND). Actions execute. Triggers dynamically enabled/disabled. **Already implemented in our EventBridge.**

18. **JASS (Just Another Scripting Syntax)** — Event-driven scripting language. All GUI compiles to JASS. Native functions form the engine API. Variables with arrays (~8000 index).

19. **Two-Layer Damage** — Layer 1: Attack Type vs Defense Type multiplier. Layer 2: Damage Type controls armor reduction. Every damage instance has both.

20. **7 Attack Types** — Normal (melee), Piercing (ranged), Siege (buildings), Magic (casters), Chaos (creeps/heroes), Spells (abilities), Hero (hero auto-attack).

21. **6 Armor Types** — Light, Medium, Heavy, Fortified, Hero, Unarmored, Divine. AT × DT = damage multiplier (Siege ×1.5 vs Fortified, Piercing ×2.0 vs Light).

22. **Damage Pipeline** — Base = DB + Dice Roll → × AT/DT multiplier → − Armor reduction (if damage type is armor-affected) → Apply to HP.

23. **Armor Formula** — Positive: `DR = (armor × 0.06) / (1 + 0.06 × armor)`. Negative: `DI = 2 - 0.94^(-armor)`.

24. **5 Weapon Types** — Normal (single), Artillery (splash + attack ground + projectile), Missile Splash (splash + projectile), Missile Bounce (chain), Missile Line (line AOE).

25. **6 Damage Types** — Normal (armor-reduced), Enhanced Physical (ignores some armor), Magic (bypasses armor), Universal (full always), Fire/Cold/Poison (DOT).

26. **Hero System** — Str=HP, Agi=armor/speed, Int=mana. Primary attribute +1 damage. Level up, 4 abilities, 6 inventory slots.

27. **Aura System** — Passive area buffs that persist while unit lives. Range-based. Multiple auras stack. Types: Damage, Armor, Speed, Regeneration, Vampiric.

28. **Splash Formula** — Full center → Medium % → Small %. Each zone independently rolled.

29. **Defend Ability** — +5 armor vs ranged, −2 vs melee, 40% block. Tactical rock-paper-scissors.

30. **Mana Shield** — HP→Mana conversion at 1:1. Creates tanking for casters.

31. **Ethereal State** — Immune to physical, +66% magic damage taken. Phase-shifting.

32. **Reincarnation** — Death → check reincarnation → revive at % HP → or process death sequence.

33. **Minimum Damage Rule** — Damage cannot be reduced below 1. Disease Cloud always deals at least 1 HP damage even to heroes.

#### Age of Empires II (17)

34. **3-Tier Pathfinding** — High: mip-mapped passability grid (ignores moving units). Mid: polygonal with dynamic convex hulls. Short: tile-based + formation flow.

35. **Frame Delay** — Frames of attack animation before projectile spawns. Effective FD = (base FD × anim duration × 60) / total frames. Kipchaks = 0, Cav Archers = 48.

36. **4 Formations** — Line (wide front), Box (360° defense), Staggered (anti-splash), Flank (cavalry sides). Units auto-order: cavalry → infantry → archers → siege.

37. **4 Stances** — Aggressive (chase LOS), Defensive (leash range), Stand Ground (never move, become wall), No Attack (ignore).

38. **Melee/Pierce Armor** — Separate values. Melee attacks vs melee armor, ranged vs pierce armor. Different from WC3 type system.

39. **Attack Tags** — Infantry, cavalry, archer, siege, ship, etc. Counter multipliers reference tags. Spearman +15 vs cavalry.

40. **Projectile Duplication** — Bug/feature: projectiles duplicate at certain angles while moving.

41. **Tech Tree (4 Ages)** — Dark → Feudal → Castle → Imperial. Each unlocks buildings, units, upgrades. 42 civs with unique gaps.

42. **7 Blacksmith Upgrades** — Attack (+melee/+ranged), Armor (melee/pierce/barding). Progressive: Feudal→Castle→Imperial tiers.

43. **Economy Tech Chain** — Wood: Double-Bit → Bow Saw → Two-Man Saw (+20% each). Farm: Horse Collar → Heavy Plow → Crop Rotation (+33%/+66%/+83%).

44. **4 Resources** — Food, Wood, Gold, Stone. Finite mines. Trade generates gold.

45. **Population Cap** — 200 max. Military competes with villagers (economic vs military tradeoff).

46. **Unique Units** — Each civ has 1-2 unique units + 2 unique techs (Castle + Imperial).

47. **Formation Splitting** — Units split around obstacles, then reform. Gaps from dead units confuse the pathfinder.

48. **Auto Scout** — Scout auto-explores, avoids enemies, returns on attack.

49. **Unique Tech: Warwolf** — Briton trebuchets deal blast damage AOE instead of single-target.

50. **Town Bell** — All villagers garrison in TC. TC fires arrows per garrisoned villager.

## NPC MECHANICS DEEP STUDY — Worker / Creep / Villager AI

### SC2 WORKER AI (SCV / Probe / Drone)

**Worker Priority System:**
- Workers have "Default Acquire Level" = **DEFENSIVE** (not OFFENSIVE like combat units)
- Defensive acquire: workers do NOT auto-acquire targets for their weapons
- Defensive acquire: workers are NOT classified as threats by other units (invisible to auto-targeting)
- Workers only attack when explicitly ordered OR when attack-moving
- AI players treat workers as ATP=10 (lower priority than buildings at ATP=11, units at ATP=20) — intentional balance favor to human opponents

**Gather/Return Loop:**
- SC2: 5 minerals per trip, 4 vespene gas per trip. MULE: 30 minerals per trip (3x longer).
- Workers auto-transition: mine patch → return to town hall → repeat
- Idle worker detection: F1 key selects idle workers. Button appears when any worker idle.
- Workers auto-idle when nearest mineral patch depletes → must be manually reassigned
- No auto-reassign to new resource — player must manage

**Repair System (SCV/MULE):**
- Auto-repair toggle: SCVs auto-repair mechanical units/buildings in range
- SCV inherits threat level + ATP of whatever it's repairing (attackers prioritize SCV if closer)
- Full repair cost = 25% of unit value (minerals + gas based on build cost)
- Multiple SCVs = faster repair (time inversely proportional). Only 1 SCV builds.
- Cost of repair does NOT increase with more SCVs — only speeds up
- MULEs repair at same rate/cost as SCVs

**Build System:**
- SCVs: must stay on building until complete (can cancel for control)
- Probes: place warp beacon → free to move. Building must be in pylon power.
- Drones: sacrifice self → morph into building. Must be on creep (except hatchery/extractor).
- Only 1 worker can construct a given building at a time (balance constraint)

### SC2 UNIT AUTO-ACQUIRE AI (Automatic Targeting)

**4-Tier Priority System:**

1. **Threats** — Units that can return fire + special flagged units:
   - Flagged "Always a Threat": High Templar, Oracle, Disruptor, Widow Mine, Raven, Lurker (unburrowed), Infestor, Swarm Host, Viper
   - Any unit whose weapon can target the attacker
   
2. **Attack Target Priority (ATP)** — Fixed per type:
   - Most units: 20
   - Most buildings: 11
   - Widow Mine (unburrowed): 19
   - Interceptor: 19
   - Larva/Cocoons: 10
   - Workers (vs AI): 10 (override)
   - Static defense (Cannon/Turret/Bunker/Planetary): 20
   
3. **Multiple Weapons** — Units with both ground + air weapons prefer primary (usually anti-air):
   - Tempest, Thor, Battlecruiser, Queen, Infested Terran
   
4. **Closest Target** — Only if no previous target. Some units use closest angle (Thor, Hellbat, Battlecruiser, Ultralisk) instead of closest distance

**Target Lock Behavior:**
- Once acquired, target locked until: dies, goes invisible, leaves weapon range, or higher-priority target enters scan range
- Scan range = max(5, weapon range) in Wings of Liberty, weapon range + 0.5 in Legacy of the Void
- Static defense always uses exactly weapon range

**Stop vs Hold Position:**
- **Stop**: Go idle → acquire target → attack → attack → attack (faster consecutive shots)
- **Hold Position**: Acquire target → attack → stop → attack → stop (faster first shot, slower consecutive)
- HP units become impassable physical objects

### WC3 CREEP AI (Neutral Hostile)

**Camp Behavior:**
- Entire camp acts as ONE unit. When one attacked, ALL attack. Cannot pull individuals.
- Creeps evaluate spellcasting every 1-2 seconds
- Camps fight together as group — weakest and strongest units coordinate

**Sleeping (Night) System:**
- At night, most creeps sleep (ZZZ indicator above head)
- Sleeping creeps: aggro range = 0. Do NOT auto-acquire.
- Wake triggers: attacked, building constructed nearby, neutral building used nearby
- Some creeps never sleep and will wake the camp if approached

**Leash / De-aggro:**
- If creep moves >400 units from camp origin → loses aggro after 5 seconds → returns to camp
- 5-second timer resets each time creep moves 400+ from camp
- Result: average chase distance = ~1750-2200 units (400 base + 5s × speed)
- Cannot be re-aggroed until fully returned to camp spot
- If re-aggroed within 3 seconds of de-aggro, the new aggro only lasts 3 seconds (not 5)

**Level-Based Intelligence:**
- **Level 1-5**: Basic — attack nearest, minimal spell use
- **Level 6+**: Hero magic resistance — negative spells have shorter duration
- **Level 7+**: Smart AI — prioritize injured units over healthy, prioritize heroes in range, attack buildings under construction, deal Chaos damage (100% vs all armor), run from towers below 60% HP

**Flying Unit Interaction:**
- Non-combat creeps ignore flying units (move command passes safely)
- Must attack-move to trigger aggro from air
- Patch 1.10: flying units using plain move command no longer aggro creeps

### AoE2 VILLAGER AI

**Task State Machine:**
- Villager title changes based on task: Villager (default/fight), Builder, Repairer, Food gatherer, Wood gatherer, Gold miner, Stone miner, Herder, Pearler
- New task instantly reassigns role — even mid-carry (keeps carried resources until drop-off)
- Gathering rates differ per resource and source (hunting fastest, farming slowest)

**Auto-Farm System:**
- Toggle: when food sources near TC exhausted, villagers auto-build farms
- If not enough wood for farm → auto-switch to wood gathering until wood available
- Farm placement near nearest Mill or Town Center

**Villager Priority (Xbox/Console):**
- Ratio-based distribution across 4 resources (Food/Wood/Gold/Stone)
- 7 presets: Food & Wood, Basic, Militia Rush, Balanced, Economic, Defensive, Naval
- Manual mode: player sets exact ratio (0-20 per resource, all ≤20)
- Newly trained villagers auto-assigned per ratio
- Not as optimal as manual micro (confirmed by devs)

**AI Opponent Villager Limits (Hardcoded):**
- Moderate AI: ~38 villagers cap
- Hard AI: ~78 villagers cap  
- Hardest AI: ~100 villagers cap
- Extreme AI: 130+ villagers cap
- AI scripted build orders: "6 on sheep, 3 on wood, villager 10 gets boar"
- AI will delete villagers for military space at pop cap

### Key Insight for Our Void NPCs

Our **ships** should use SC2's **4-tier auto-acquire** system (Threat→ATP→Weapon→Closest), combined with WC3's **leash/chase** behavior (ships have patrol radius around CPL, chase enemies up to X distance, then return to course). Workers/resource gatherers should use AoE2's **task state machine** with SC2's **defensive acquire** (ignore enemies unless attack-moving).
